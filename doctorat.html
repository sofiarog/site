Ph.D. & Research

Ph.D. & in few words...

My thesis, Les limites planétaires territoriales (Territorial Planetary Boundaries), asks how limits designed for the planet as a whole can be translated to the scale of territories, without ignoring what makes each place ecologically specific. I am currently in my fourth year.

Research Question

The planetary boundaries framework defines nine global limits, but public action happens in regions, cities and rural areas. My thesis questions how these limits can be transposed to territories while taking into account the intrinsic ecological thresholds of each space.

Rather than simply downscaling the nine boundaries, I propose a bottom-up approach to identifying endogenous carrying capacities. It rests on a systemic framework linking biophysical metabolisms, social dynamics and governance arrangements.

The thesis has three parts. The first builds a conceptual architecture of “limits” in economic traditions and traces their transformation from Meadows to Rockström, from the register of forecasting to that of precaution. The second combines a systematic bibliometric analysis with a multicriteria grid to assess territorialisation methods, then develops a typology of territorial limits supported by case studies. The third applies this work to the Hauts-de-France region, exploring the relevant ecological thresholds and their implications for public action.

Keywords: planetary boundaries · ecological limits · sustainability science · ecological economics. Thesis record on theses.fr
